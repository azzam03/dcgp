01 / The Problem

Classification work still happens
in scattered Excel files.

Speak to almost any data governance team or consultant and the same picture emerges: hundreds of systems, thousands of fields, long review cycles, and an Excel sheet that nobody fully trusts. Below are the recurring frictions this platform was built to address.

01

Volume that overwhelms

Large numbers of systems and database fields make manual review slow, inconsistent, and exhausting to maintain.

02

Spreadsheet-driven process

Classification lives in Excel files passed between owners, often without versioning, validation, or audit trails.

03

Endless review meetings

Cycles of meetings to debate the same fields again and again, with little structure carried between sessions.

04

Personal data is hard to spot

Identifying potential personal and sensitive fields requires context that scattered spreadsheets simply do not preserve.

05

Weak privacy readiness

Teams enter governance and privacy reviews without a clear, structured view of what data they actually hold.

06

Heavy consultant dependency

Manual interpretation by external consultants becomes the bottleneck — and the single source of expertise.

02 / Market Need

Why the market needs
a tool like this.

Data governance, privacy readiness, and data classification have moved from optional initiatives into core organizational priorities. Organizations cannot govern, protect, or responsibly use data they have not properly understood — and that understanding starts at the metadata layer.

Yet most teams still rely on manual spreadsheets, repeated reviews, and informal handoffs. That gap leaves real room for practical, lightweight tools that support classification and governance workflows without the weight of an enterprise platform rollout.

Recurring market signals

  • Governance Increasing focus on knowing what data exists before applying controls.
  • Privacy Growing pressure to identify and review personal data fields proactively.
  • Operations Demand for lighter tools that fit consulting and project-based work.
  • Reporting Need for structured, presentable outputs for review committees.

04 / How It Works

From upload to report,
in five clear steps.

The workflow is intentionally linear. Each step produces an artifact the next step builds on — so progress is visible, reviewable, and easy to hand over.

  1. 01

    Create System

    Register the system, capture context, and prepare it to receive metadata for classification.

  2. 02

    Upload Metadata File

    Import an Excel or CSV file containing database fields, descriptions, and supporting context.

  3. 03

    Run AI Classification

    The platform suggests confidentiality levels and flags fields that may contain personal data.

  4. 04

    Review & PDPL Notes

    Reviewers verify suggestions, approve personal data records, and attach governance notes.

  5. 05

    Export Final Report

    Generate a structured, styled Excel report ready for governance review and documentation.

Field-level classification suggestions generated from metadata context.

PDPL Review Page

Professional Excel Export

System Context & Links

Dashboard & Progress Tracking

A clear overview of where each system sits in the classification workflow.
